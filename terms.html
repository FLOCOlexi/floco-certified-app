Surface preparation & dust notice

  • Installation requires mechanical surface preparation, including roughening of the existing substrate.
  • This process may generate dust and debris. We take extensive precautions, including vacuum-assisted dust collection, to minimize impact and maintain a clean job site.
  • Some dust migration may still occur and is considered normal and unavoidable during surface preparation.

Construction process

  • Granulated rubber surfacing is a non-invasive installation process and typically does not require demolition or major construction tear-outs.
  • Minor disruptions may occur during installation, but we strive to minimize inconvenience to the property and occupants.

Maintenance & UV protection

  • Granulated rubber surfacing requires a UV protective coating to preserve color, appearance and long-term performance.
  • We offer professional UV coating and maintenance services, which are strongly recommended.
  • Failure to maintain the surface, including recommended UV coating services, may result in premature fading or wear and may affect warranty coverage.
