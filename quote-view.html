why TPV, not EPDM

We use premium TPV rubber for outdoor projects because of its UV stability, durability and long-term performance in this climate. It holds its colour, it stays flexible instead of going brittle, and it leaves far less powder residue than EPDM, so the binder coats the granule properly. Half an inch thick, hand-troweled, never sprayed.
